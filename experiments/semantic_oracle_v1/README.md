# Semantic Oracle Diagnostic Benchmark (`semantic_oracle_v1`)

> **Scope:** Validation-only (`2025-10-01` through `2026-02-18`), `heldout_access = false`, `training_executed = false`, `promoted = false`, `metadata_policy = snapshot_unverified`. Held-out Test (`>= 2026-02-19`) is strictly untouched and date-gated before DataFrame materialization.

## 1. Objective & Scientific Framing

This diagnostic benchmark evaluates a tool-backed blind LLM expert on matched historical B2B next-basket candidate slates to answer two questions:

1. **Where does additional predictive signal exist** beyond the current neural and tree models (cross-company trajectories, unbounded customer history beyond 32 events, or semantic/domain procurement reasoning)?
2. **Which error modes dominate** (Stage 1 candidate coverage vs. Stage 2 ranking vs. missing cross-company/semantic features vs. weak historical evidence), and which data-augmentation or feature-engineering strategies merit controlled training experiments?

**Critical methodological caveat:** LLM performance is an empirical expert benchmark, **not** a mathematical Bayes-optimal upper bound. True candidate-constrained theoretical ceilings (`OracleRecall@10`, `OracleNDCG@10`, independent new/repeat ceilings) are computed separately per case.

## 2. Repository-Grounded Architecture & Valid Baselines

The benchmark runs two strictly separated, exact candidate-set comparisons on the **same 50 stratified validation transitions** (`seed = 42`, 50 unique customers across 41 industries and 5 validation months, deliberately oversampling discovery/coverage strata; unweighted sample metrics are exploratory and not full-population estimates):

- **Compatible baselines evaluated on exact same 50 candidates:**
  - `upscanr_ndcg`: `checkpoints/ranker/best_ranker.pt` (Epoch 7, `SHA-256: 1701becf...`, `frozen_train_cutoff_v1` statistics, legacy 13 pairwise features + 32-event `UPSCANRRanker`).
  - `upscanr_discovery`: `checkpoints/ranker/best_discovery_ranker.pt` (Epoch 4, `SHA-256: 6314f050...`).
  - `personal_repeat_prior`: Point-in-time personal repeat frequency/recency heuristic with global popularity tie-breaking restricted strictly to the 50 candidates.
  - `popularity_prior`: Point-in-time global popularity strictly before `t` restricted to the 50 candidates.

## 3. Controlled Information Ablations & Oracle Model Selection

Each of the 50 cases × 2 benchmarks is evaluated under 3 physically separated blind arms (plus 5 stability replicates on `rolling100 / global_history`, totaling **305 isolated prediction sessions**):

| Arm | Physical Data Bundle | Global Tools | Descriptive Product Text |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `customer_only` | Focal company purchases `< t` only | Physically absent & rejected | Included |
| `global_history` | All companies' paid purchases `< t` | Enabled (`similar_companies`, `relations`, `trajectories`, `product_history`, `market_trends`, `company_history`) | Included |
| `reduced_semantic` | All companies' paid purchases `< t` | Enabled | Stripped (`short_name`, `ukrainian_name`, `product_name`, `base_name` physically removed) |

### Model Selection Rationale (November 2026 Benchmark & Quota Audit)

The initial `gemini-4-hydrogen` (`thinking_level: high`) pilot hit Antigravity quota before sealing its first session due to oversized `context` JSON output (~48.6 KB triggering 9 re-reads) and verbose thinking token burn. Before sealing any predictions, we:

1. **Fixed gateway I/O overhead** in [`gateway.py`](../../recommender/experiments/semantic_oracle/gateway.py) and [`history_tools.py`](../../recommender/experiments/semantic_oracle/history_tools.py): compact lossless context/query views capped at 9,000 chars per stdout emission with safe session-local `view` pagination, optional `candidate_only` display filtering on `relations` (preserving complete-market denominators), and one-shot `submit --stdin`.
2. **Selected `gpt-6.2-sol` (`reasoningEffort: medium`, provider `codex`)** as the uniform oracle model across all sessions based on current November 2026 benchmarks:
   - **GPT-6.2 Sol:** ~5× lower token usage per task than Gemini 4 (`60.9M` vs `283–304M` tokens on AA Index), strong analytical/STEM reasoning (`57` AA Intelligence Index, `60.5%` Terminal-Bench-Science at `medium`, June 2026 knowledge cutoff), and zero long-context surcharge for our `< 20K`-token sessions.
   - **Gemini 4 Neon (`60–61` AA Index):** Highest agentic coding/enterprise benchmark score, retained for orchestration/analysis while avoiding verbose high-volume token burn across 305 blind sessions.
   - **Gemini 4 Hydrogen (`51–54` AA Index):** Lowest per-token Gemini list rate, but trails both Neon and Sol on analytical reasoning and drops sharply below `high` effort.

## 4. Temporal Integrity, Blinding & Protocol Guarantees

- **Strict `purchase_date < t` gate:** Enforced both during blind snapshot export (`data.py`) and inside every `HistoryTools` query (`_past()`). Same-day purchases (`== t`) from focal or other companies and held-out rows (`>= 2026-02-19`) are excluded and unit-tested (`tests/test_semantic_oracle.py`).
- **Pseudonymization & blinding:** Company IDs are hashed (`C-...`), candidate ordering is deterministically shuffled per `(case_id, benchmark)`, and targets/baseline scores live exclusively in `data/interim/semantic_oracle_v1/vault/truth.json` (git-ignored, `0600`).
- **Global reveal barrier:** `evaluate.py` refuses to compute metrics or create `reveal.json` until every planned session has a valid `seal.json` with intact prediction/session/tool-log hashes and `runtime_receipts.json` confirms `access_audit_status == "passed"`.
- **Snapshot metadata caveat:** Company and product catalogue metadata are snapshot-based (`metadata_policy = snapshot_unverified`) rather than effective-dated; retrospective fields such as customer purchase counts are stripped before export, and this limitation is explicitly carried into all interpretations.
